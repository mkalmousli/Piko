# Privacy

Piko does not collect, store off-device, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics,
advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

**On-device only**
- App launch history (used to rank search results), tags, hidden apps, layout and settings are stored in
  Piko's private app storage and never leave your phone.
- Contacts are read only if you grant the permission, are indexed in memory for search, and are never
  uploaded.

**Network use**
- *Currency conversion*: when you type something like `100 usd to eur`, Piko requests
  `https://api.frankfurter.app` with the amount and the two currency codes. Nothing else is sent.
- *Web & AI search*: tapping an engine button opens your browser / that service with your query. That
  is governed by the service you choose.

Questions: open an [issue](https://github.com/mkalmousli/Piko/issues) or see [al-mo.de](https://al-mo.de).
